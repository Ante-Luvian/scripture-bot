# ScriptureBot changelog

What's new, newest first. When the bot starts after an update, she posts any
entries a server hasn't seen yet to that server's changelog channel
(`!changelogsetup #channel`); anyone can read recent ones with `!changelog`.

Format: one `## YYYY-MM-DD · Title` section per notable change (new commands,
new features, fixes users would notice), with `- ` bullets written for users.
Once an entry has been announced, don't change its heading — the heading is
how the bot remembers what it has already posted.

Write every bullet for someone who has never seen the code or this project's
history (see CLAUDE.md): one change per bullet; say in plain words what's new
or what was wrong; give an example command to try; explain any term a casual
reader wouldn't know.

## 2026-10-12 · The Gospel of Thomas, the Enuma Elish & more
- 📜 **The Gospel of Thomas.** The famous collection of 114 sayings of Jesus, found buried in Egypt in 1945 among the Nag Hammadi books: `!verse Gospel of Thomas 77` (or just `!verse Thomas 77`). This is a different book from the *Infancy* Gospel of Thomas I added earlier, the stories of Jesus as a boy.
- 🌙 **The Enuma Elish.** The Babylonian epic of creation, in which the god Marduk defeats the sea-dragon Tiamat and builds the world from her body, in its seven tablets: `!verse Enuma Elish 4`.
- ☸️ **The Sutta Nipāta.** One of the oldest collections of the Buddha's teachings, including the Metta Sutta on loving-kindness. Look up a whole discourse by its section (`!verse Sutta Nipata 1.8`) or a single verse by its number (`!verse Sutta Nipata 143`).
- 👼 **More ancient writings:** the **Odes of Solomon**, 42 early Christian hymns (`!verse Odes of Solomon 1`), and the First and Second **Books of Adam and Eve**, the old story of Adam and Eve's life after Eden and their struggle with Satan (`!verse First Book of Adam and Eve 1`).

## 2026-10-12 · Look up a stretch of Plato
- 🏛️ **Plato ranges.** You can now ask for several sections of a dialogue at once, the way Plato's text is usually quoted: `!verse Republic 361e-362a`. A range can stay on one page (`!verse Republic 514a-c`, the start of the Allegory of the Cave) or cover whole pages (`!verse Republic 514-515`), and long passages come a page at a time. Before, a range just said the reference couldn't be found.

## 2026-10-12 · A much bigger library: Buddhism, China, the Gita, Dante & more
- ☯️ **New oracle: `!iching`.** The *I Ching* (Book of Changes) is the ancient Chinese way of asking for guidance. I toss three coins six times to build a "hexagram", a figure of six lines, then read you its Judgment and Image from James Legge's classic translation. Any "changing" lines get their own reading and turn your hexagram into a second one, which I show too. Add a question if you like: `!iching should I take the job?`
- 🌹 **New oracle: `!hafez`.** In Iran people still consult Hafiz, the beloved 14th-century Persian poet: you hold a wish or question in mind and open his book of poems at random, and the poem you find is your answer. I'll open it for you (43 of his poems, in Gertrude Bell's translation). Both new oracles have a **Card** button, and admins can choose them for the daily oracle (`!oraclesetup kind iching` or `hafez`).
- ☸️ **Buddhism.** The **Dhammapada**, the Buddha's teachings in 423 short verses: look one up by its number, like `!verse Dhammapada 183`. Also the **Diamond Sutra**, a famous Mahayana teaching on emptiness.
- 🏮 **The Chinese classics.** Laozi's **Tao Te Ching** (`!verse Tao Te Ching 1`), Confucius's **Analects**, cited the usual way by book and chapter (`!verse Analects 2.4`), and the full text of the **I Ching**.
- 🕉️ **Hinduism beyond the Vedas.** The **Bhagavad Gita**, every verse of all 18 chapters (`!verse Gita 2:47`), and three of the **Upanishads**, the philosophical heart of the Vedas: the Isa, Katha and Kena (`!verse Katha 1:20`).
- 🏛️ **The Stoics and Hesiod.** Marcus Aurelius's **Meditations** (`!verse Meditations 4.3`), Epictetus's **Enchiridion**, a short handbook for a calm life (`!verse Epictetus 1`), and Hesiod's **Works and Days** (advice on farming and honest living, by line: `!verse Works and Days 289`) and **Shield of Heracles**.
- ⛪ **Medieval classics.** Dante's **Divine Comedy** in Longfellow's translation, by canto and line, as it's usually cited (`!verse Inferno 1.1`, `!verse Paradiso 33.145`); **The Imitation of Christ** by Thomas à Kempis (`!verse Imitation of Christ 1.1`); and the **Rule of St Benedict**, the guide that shaped Western monastic life.
- 📜 **New Testament Apocrypha.** 41 early Christian writings that didn't make it into the Bible, including the **Protevangelium of James** (the story of Mary's birth and childhood: `!verse Protevangelium 1:1`), the **Infancy Gospel of Thomas** (stories of Jesus as a boy), the **Gospel of Nicodemus** with Christ's descent into hell (`!verse Gospel of Nicodemus 1:1`), and the **Acts of Paul and Thecla**. Plus **2 Enoch**, the *Book of the Secrets of Enoch*.
- 🎯 **A harder `!quiz hard`.** Its mix of traditions now includes the Dhammapada, Tao Te Ching, Analects, Bhagavad Gita, Hafiz, Marcus Aurelius and the Imitation of Christ.
- 📚 `!books` has the new texts, with a new "Eastern Traditions" section for the Vedas, the Upanishads, the Gita, the Buddhist texts and the Chinese classics.

## 2026-10-12 · Better Homer and Plato
- 📜 **Homer's *Iliad* and *Odyssey* work with `!verse` now, in a better translation.** Looking up Homer used to fail with "has no chapter". Now you can look him up the way scholars cite him, by book and line: `!verse Iliad 6.440` shows that passage, `!verse Iliad 6.440-465` a longer stretch, and `!verse Odyssey 1` the whole first book, a page at a time. The text is now A. T. Murray's translation for the Loeb Classical Library, a close, line-by-line rendering of the Greek, in place of an older prose version that was labelled wrongly and had stray "[ ]" marks scattered through it. Hundreds of scanning errors in the *Iliad* have been corrected too (for example "Loerians" for Locrians).
- 🏛️ **Plato's dialogues are cut in the right places, and credit the right translators.** Plato is cited by "Stephanus numbers", the page-and-section numbers of a 1578 edition that every modern book prints in its margins: `!verse Republic 514a` is the start of the Allegory of the Cave. My sections used to run on into the next one, some were missing, and the *Laws* and the *Letters* couldn't be looked up at all: `!verse Laws 624a` said "not found". All of that works now. Every dialogue also names its actual translator (Fowler, Lamb, Bury or Shorey) instead of saying "Jowett" for all of them. Plato's *Apology* (Socrates' defence speech at his trial) was even credited to "Rufinus", the author of a different book with the same name.

## 2026-10-12 · Learn verses by heart, a daily quiz & more
- 🧠 **New: `!memorize`, for learning a verse by heart.** Type `!memorize John 3:16` and I'll show the verse with some of its words blanked out. Say it aloud, filling in the gaps, then press **Reveal** to check yourself (only you see the answer). Press **Harder** to hide more words, until only the first letter of each word is left; **Easier** goes back. When you've got it, press **🔔 Remind me** and I'll send it to you by private message to practise again after 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days. Reviewing at growing intervals like this is a well-known way to make things stick. `!memorize list` shows your verses, and `!memorize stop John 3:16` stops one.
- 📅 **A daily quiz.** Server admins can now have me post one quiz question a day, like the daily verse: `!quizsetup channel #your-channel` (choose the time with `!quizsetup time 18:00`, 24-hour UTC, and make it harder with `!quizsetup kind hard`). Everyone has all day to make one guess, and the answer is revealed when the next day's question arrives.
- 🏆 **Quiz scores now belong to each server, and start fresh every week.** `!quiz leaderboard` used to mix together players from every server I'm in, so you could see names from servers you've never visited. Now it only shows this server's players, with two lists: **this week** (reset every Monday) and **all time**.
- 🔥 **Quiz streaks.** Get three or more answers right in a row and I'll tell you your streak. Your best run shows on the leaderboard.
- 🖱️ **Look up the verses in any message.** Right-click a message (on a phone, press and hold it), choose **Apps**, then **Look up verses**, and I'll show you every Bible reference in it, up to five, privately, just for you. Handy for a long list of verses someone posted, or for older messages from before I was here.
- 📖 **"Today's readings" under the Verse of the Day.** Press the new **📅 Today's readings** button under any Verse of the Day to see the readings for that day from the *Book of Common Prayer* (the traditional Anglican prayer book): the psalms and Bible passages for morning and evening prayer, and the day's prayer, called the collect. Only you see it. `!mp`, `!ep` and `!collect` show the full texts.

## 2026-10-12 · A quiz game, reading reminders & slash commands for everything
- 🎯 **New game: `!quiz`.** I show you a Bible verse and four buttons — which book is it from? Everyone gets one guess (I'll tell you privately whether you were right), and after a minute I reveal the answer and who got it. For a real challenge, `!quiz hard` mixes traditions: is it from Proverbs, the Quran, the Rig Veda, the Norse Edda, the Book of Mormon…? `!quiz leaderboard` shows the top scorers.
- 📬 **Reading-plan reminders.** If you're reading through a book with `!plan start John`, type `!plan remind on` and I'll send you each day's chapter by private message, every morning at 07:00 UTC — or choose your own time, like `!plan remind on 19:30`. `!plan remind off` stops them. (Your Discord privacy settings need to allow messages from server members.)
- ⌨️ **Every command now works as a slash command too.** Commands that could only be typed with `!` before — including `/virgil`, `/search`, `/prayer`, `/psalm`, `/mp`, `/ep`, `/collect`, `/stats` and `/export` — now appear when you type `/`, with Discord showing you what to fill in. Server admins get slash versions of the setup commands as well (`/votdsetup`, `/oraclesetup`, `/changelogsetup`, `/features`), visible only to admins.
- 📖 **This introduction keeps itself up to date.** The guide at the top of this channel now updates in place whenever my commands change, so it's always accurate.
- 👋 **A better welcome.** When I join a new server, my hello message now points admins to everything worth setting up: this introduction and updates channel, the daily verse, and the daily oracle.

## 2026-10-12 · Daily oracles, smarter verse spotting & more
- 🔮 **New: a daily oracle.** Server admins can have me post an oracle every day, like the daily verse. Start with `!oraclesetup channel #your-channel`; then optionally choose the time with `!oraclesetup time 12:00` (24-hour clock, UTC), pick which oracle with `!oraclesetup kind virgil` (or `homer`, `delphi`, `oracle`, or `any` for a surprise each day), and try it right away with `!oraclesetup test`. `!oraclesetup off` stops it.
- ❓ **Ask the oracles a question.** You could always type a question after an oracle command, but I used to ignore it. Now `!virgil will I find work?` shows your question at the top of the answer — and on the image if you press **Card** — so the answer makes sense when you share it. Works with `!virgil`, `!homer`, `!delphi`, `!oracle` and `!sortesbiblicae`.
- 💬 **I notice verses mid-sentence now.** When someone mentions a Bible reference in ordinary chat, I reply with the verse — but this only worked if the reference started the message. Now "as it says in John 3:16…" or "I keep thinking about 1 John 4:8" works too, and those replies get the **Notes · Cross-refs · Interlinear · Card** buttons as well.
- 🔘 **Buttons keep working after updates.** Every time I'm updated I restart, and the buttons on older messages used to stop responding. The study buttons and the oracle **Card** buttons now keep working on messages from before an update (from this update on).
- ⌨️ **Book suggestions in `/verse`.** As you type a reference into the `/verse` slash command, I suggest matching book names — Bible books first, then the rest of my library (handy for things like the Apostolic Fathers or the Vedas). Abbreviations work too: typing "Jn" suggests John.
- ⚠️ **If something goes wrong, I'll say so.** When a command or button fails, you'll now see a short "Sorry — something went wrong" message instead of nothing at all, and my owner gets told so it can be fixed.
- 📊 **`!stats` shows proper book names.** The "most looked-up books" list was counting books by their first word only, so "1 Corinthians" showed up as "1" and "Book of Enoch" as "Book". It now shows the full names.

## 2026-10-12 · The big update: notes, study buttons, new oracle & more
- 📝 **New command: `!notes`.** The NET Bible comes with thousands of notes from its translators explaining why a word was translated the way it was, what the original Greek or Hebrew says, and where ancient manuscripts disagree. Type `!notes John 3:16` to see them for any Bible verse (or up to 5 verses at once, like `!notes Genesis 1:1-3`). Small numbers in the verse show which word each note is about.
- 🔘 **Buttons under Bible verses.** When you look up a Bible verse with `!verse`, four buttons now appear underneath: **Notes** shows the translators' notes above, **Cross-refs** lists related verses elsewhere in the Bible, **Interlinear** shows the verse word by word in the original Greek or Hebrew, and **Card** turns the verse into an image you can share. Each button works once per message.
- 📖 **New oracle: `!sortesbiblicae`** (or the shorter `!bibliomancy`). An old tradition: open the Bible at a random page and read the verse your eye falls on as an answer. I'll pick a random verse from anywhere in the Bible for you, and it comes with the same four buttons.
- 🖼️ **Oracle answers as pictures.** Answers from `!virgil`, `!homer`, `!delphi` and `!oracle` now have a **Card** button that turns the answer into an image, like `!card` does for verses.
- 📜 **I'll announce my own updates** in a channel the server's admins choose with `!changelogsetup #channel` — that's what this message is! Anyone can type `!changelog` to see recent updates.
- 🔧 **Fixed: the ancient Near Eastern texts.** `!verse Gilgamesh 1`, `!verse Baal 1:1`, `!verse Aten` and the rest of that collection (the Epic of Gilgamesh, the Baal Cycle, the Hymn to the Aten, the Book of the Dead, the Mu'allaqat poems…) were showing errors or garbled text. They display properly again.
- 🔧 **Fixed: comparing translations.** Asking for one verse in several translations at once, like `!verse John 3:16 kjv web`, was failing with an error. It now shows them side by side again.
- 🔧 **Fixed: the Shepherd of Hermas.** This early Christian book is divided into Visions, Mandates and Similitudes, and you can now look it up the way scholars cite it: `!verse Herm Sim 9:1` means Similitude 9, chapter 1 (and `Herm Vis` and `Herm Man` work the same way). These used to open the wrong passages.
- 🔧 **Fixed: typing a Mishnah reference the way I display it.** (The Mishnah is the earliest written collection of Jewish oral law, from around AD 200.) Its passages are labelled like "Mishnah Berakhot 1:1", but typing that label back in, as in `!verse Mishnah Berakhot 1:1`, said "Unknown book". It works now, as does the short form `!verse Berakhot 1:1`.
- 🔧 **Fixed: verse ranges with spaces.** Typing a range with spaces around the dash, like `!verse John 3:16 - 18`, used to say "Unknown book". It now works just like `John 3:16-18`.
- 🔧 **Fixed: Psalms 151–155** (extra psalms found in some Bibles) were labelled oddly, like "Psalm 151 151:1". Now they read "Psalm 151:1".
